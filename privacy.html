03The Orrery waitlist

If you join the waitlist, your email address is sent to a function I run on Google Cloud (Google Cloud Functions, region europe-west1, Belgium) and stored with the product's name and the time you joined in Google Cloud Firestore (region europe-west3, Frankfurt). I use it only to write to you when Orrery is ready. It is not used for anything else.

When you send the form, the address is kept as unconfirmed and you get one email with a link. Only when you press the button on that page are you on the list; I keep the times you asked and confirmed, to be able to show your consent. An address that is not confirmed is deleted after about 7 days. People who joined before 25 November 2026 were not asked to confirm. The confirmation email is sent through Resend, described next.

When you confirm, a notice with your email address, the product's name and the number of people on the list is sent to my own inbox (Gmail, Google Ireland Ltd.) through Resend (Plus Five Five, Inc., San Francisco, USA), an email service that processes it on my behalf under its data processing agreement. Resend processes data mainly in the USA; it states that it is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and also uses the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. See Resend's privacy policy. Your address is not shared with anyone else.

Legal basis: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. Keeping unconfirmed addresses for those days, the proof of your consent and the daily limits against abuse (counters without addresses) rest on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest). You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing me or with the contact form, and I delete your address and the notice. Otherwise I delete the list and the notices once I have written to everyone on it that Orrery is ready.

When you send the form, Google also processes your IP address and request data in its logs to run the function and keep it secure. Google (Google Ireland Ltd., Dublin; data may also be processed by Google LLC, USA) processes this on my behalf under its data processing terms; transfers to the USA rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

04North Star

North Star is a collaborative workspace that needs an account. Its data is stored with Google Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd.; Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage in Frankfurt, Realtime Database and Cloud Functions in Belgium):

  • Signing in: with your Google account (Firebase Authentication, whose account data Google may process in the USA). North Star receives your name, email address, profile picture and a user ID from Google, and keeps your sign-in in your browser's storage.
  • What you make: the workspaces, canvases, cards, documents, files, comments and tasks you and the people you share them with create, and who is a member of which workspace.
  • Presence: while you are in a workspace, the other members see that you are there, where your cursor is and what you have selected.
  • Settings and what you have seen: your display choices, and your own AI key and its usage if you use one, are kept in your browser; which tips you have seen is also kept with your account.
  • Your browser's storage: North Star uses local storage and IndexedDB for your sign-in, your settings and an offline copy of your workspaces, and the Firebase library keeps a small record of its own there (its sign-in store and its "heartbeat" of library versions) already when the page opens. There are no advertising or analytics cookies. I consider this storage strictly necessary for the service you ask for (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).
  • Technical data: Google processes your IP address and request data when North Star reads or saves data, to deliver and secure the service.
  • The AI assistant: optional. With your own OpenAI or Anthropic API key, your browser sends your requests and the workspace content they need straight to that provider, under your own agreement with it; your key stays in your browser. If the agent searches the web, the provider runs the search, and the search terms can contain workspace content. With a workspace key the owners set, requests go through a function I run on Google Cloud to OpenAI (OpenAI Ireland Ltd.; possibly OpenAI, L.L.C., USA), and the number of requests and tokens per person is logged, never the messages. That key belongs to whoever set it: OpenAI processes the request under that account's agreement with OpenAI, whose API terms say that API data is not used to train its models by default.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the service you signed up for) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (keeping it secure). You can use the app from the age of 16. Your data is kept while your account and your workspaces exist; deleting a workspace deletes its content. You can delete your account in Settings → account: that deletes it, the workspaces only you are in, and your private documents and chats elsewhere; what you made in shared workspaces stays for the others without your name, except where a name was typed into a text. Files can take about a day to go, and backups at Google some time longer. Google processes data on my behalf under its data processing terms; transfers to the USA rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified under it, and otherwise on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.
