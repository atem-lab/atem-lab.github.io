Privacy policy

Datenschutzerklärung · atem-lab.com

This policy covers atem-lab.com: the home page, the Orrery waitlist, and North Star at atem-lab.com/north-star. The home page sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking, and loads its fonts from this site rather than from Google.

01Controller

Atahan Kılıç, address as in the Impressum, atahankilic.dev@gmail.com

02Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you open a page, GitHub processes your IP address and technical request data (browser, time, requested file) in its server logs to deliver the site and keep it secure. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in operating a secure website). GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See GitHub's privacy statement.

03The Orrery waitlist

If you join the waitlist, your email address is sent to a function I run on Google Cloud (Google Cloud Functions, region europe-west1, Belgium) and stored with the product's name and the time you joined in Google Cloud Firestore (region europe-west3, Frankfurt). I use it only to write to you when Orrery is ready. It is not shared with anyone else and not used for anything else.

Legal basis: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. You can withdraw it at any time by emailing me, and I delete your address. Otherwise I delete the list once I have written to everyone on it that Orrery is ready.

When you send the form, Google also processes your IP address and request data in its logs to run the function and keep it secure. Google (Google Ireland Ltd., Dublin; data may also be processed by Google LLC, USA) processes this on my behalf under its data processing terms; transfers to the USA rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

04North Star

North Star is a collaborative workspace that needs an account. Its data is stored with Google Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd.; Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage in Frankfurt, Realtime Database and Cloud Functions in Belgium):

  • Signing in: with your Google account (Firebase Authentication). North Star receives your name, email address, profile picture and a user ID from Google, and keeps your sign-in in your browser's storage.
  • What you make: the workspaces, canvases, cards, documents, files, comments and tasks you and the people you share them with create, and who is a member of which workspace.
  • Presence: while you are in a workspace, the other members see that you are there, where your cursor is and what you have selected.
  • Settings: your display choices are kept in your browser.
  • The AI assistant: optional. With your own OpenAI or Anthropic API key, your browser sends your requests and the workspace content they need straight to that provider, under your own agreement with it; your key stays in your browser. With a workspace key the owners set, requests go through a function I run on Google Cloud to OpenAI (OpenAI Ireland Ltd.; possibly OpenAI, L.L.C., USA), and the number of requests and tokens per person is logged, never the messages. OpenAI does not use API data to train its models.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the service you signed up for) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (keeping it secure). Your data is kept while your account and your workspaces exist; deleting a workspace deletes its content. Google and OpenAI process data on my behalf under data processing agreements; transfers to the USA rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified under it, and otherwise on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

05Email

If you email me, I use your address and message only to reply. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR.

06Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object to processing (Art. 15–21 GDPR), and to withdraw a consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR). To use them, email me. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, for example the Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht (BayLDA).
