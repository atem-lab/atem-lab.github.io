Open North Star

Your notes, research and plan are one thing. Keep them in one place.

Start with who hurts

Put customers, problems and ideas down as cards, then link them: this problem is for them, this idea solves it. Every card shows whether it is still a hypothesis, being tested, or validated.

See what rivals leave open

Break competitors into their features and set them against yours. Score each cell, say how sure you are, and the gaps show.

Choose what to build first

Features sit by effort and impact. North Star suggests an order from impact, confidence and effort, and confidence counts how much of what a feature rests on is validated or being tested.

Be in the same room

See everyone’s cursor, follow a teammate to where they are, comment on anything and hand out tasks. Invite people as owners, editors or viewers.
